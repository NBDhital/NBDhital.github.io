What I Work On

Research Focus

My research centres on real-world emission characterization of vehicles, industrial and residential combustion sources, and on exposure and health impact assessment of ambient and indoor air pollution. I combine field measurements, portable emission monitoring systems, and epidemiological approaches to understand how air pollution affects public health across South Asia, East Asia and the Nordic region.

Core themes include tailpipe and non-exhaust vehicular emissions, in-vehicle and in-cabin air quality, wood-combustion particulate emissions, wildfire-related air pollution in the Himalayas, and estimating the health burden attributable to fine particulate matter (PM2.5).
