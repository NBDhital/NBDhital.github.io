A Vision for Clean Air

Namaste!

I'm a researcher specializing in air pollution, with nearly a decade of experience in the field. Growing up in the rural mountains of Nepal, I was accustomed to clear visibility, with the Himalayas and surrounding mountains forming a constant backdrop to daily life. At the time, I rarely considered that those unobstructed views were a reflection of the clean air that surrounded me. It was only after witnessing the growing impacts of air pollution over recent decades that I realized how precious that resource was. Since then, the haze that increasingly obscures the Himalayan views of my childhood has become a powerful reminder of not only what has been lost, but also the serious health consequences of air pollution for communities across Nepal and South Asia.

Guided by a vision of restoring cleaner air and healthier communities across Nepal and South Asia, I study the sources, dynamics, and impacts of atmospheric pollution. My research focuses on emission characterization, human exposure, and health impacts, generating the scientific evidence needed to inform effective policies and practical solutions.

I welcome opportunities to collaborate with researchers, students, policymakers, and organizations who share an interest in advancing air quality science and developing evidence-based solutions for cleaner air.
