Narayan Babu Dhital, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Department of Environmental Science
Patan Multiple Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal

I am an Assistant Professor and researcher specializing in air pollution science, committed to improving air quality across Nepal and South Asia. My work focuses on emission characterization from various sources, generating the scientific evidence needed to address one of the region's most pressing environmental challenges. Guided by a vision of restoring the clean air I breathed growing up in rural Nepal, I investigate the origins, patterns, and health impacts of atmospheric pollution to inform evidence-based policy and practical solutions. I am dedicated to mentoring the next generation of scientists while fostering collaborative research that bridges academia, policy, and practice—working toward cleaner air for Nepal and South Asia.

Research interest: Real-World Emission Characterization; Exposure and Health Impact Assessment.
