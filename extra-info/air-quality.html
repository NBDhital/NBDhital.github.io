Where These Data Come From

The live reading above comes from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API , which combines outputs from atmospheric models (including CAMS – the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service) with ground-based observations to estimate air pollutant concentrations at any location, updated hourly. Please note that it is a modeled estimate, not a direct observation. The AQI value is based on the PM2.5 concentration.

For Nepal's official air quality monitoring data, visit the Department of Environment's real-time monitoring portal.

Understanding the AQI

The Air Quality Index (AQI) is a standardized scale, used widely around the world, that translates air pollutant concentrations into a single number from 0 to 500+, making it easy to understand whether the air is healthy. A higher AQI means a greater level of air pollution and a greater health concern. The scale is built from six categories, each linked to a PM2.5 concentration range and a health message.
